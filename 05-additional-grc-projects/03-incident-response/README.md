# Enterprise Cyber Incident Response Plan

## Project Overview

This project involved developing an enterprise-wide Cyber Incident Response Plan for Oscorp Industries, a simulated organization, as part of the GRC Mastery professional training programme.

The plan establishes a structured approach to detecting, managing, escalating and recovering from cybersecurity incidents, with particular emphasis on executive involvement, stakeholder communication, regulatory compliance and organizational resilience.

## Business Problem

Following a minor data breach, Oscorp identified a significant gap in its cybersecurity incident management arrangements.

Although the organization possessed technical incident response capabilities, it lacked a documented procedure for engaging senior management and coordinating stakeholders during a significant cybersecurity incident or enterprise-wide cyber crisis.

The organization required a formal incident response plan that established clear responsibilities, escalation procedures, decision-making authority and communication requirements.

## Project Objectives

- Establish a structured enterprise-wide incident response process.
- Define incident management and executive governance responsibilities.
- Develop incident classification and escalation criteria.
- Establish internal and external stakeholder notification procedures.
- Define regulatory and contractual notification responsibilities.
- Establish evidence preservation and chain-of-custody procedures.
- Integrate incident response with business continuity and disaster recovery.
- Establish continuous improvement and incident response testing requirements.

## Solution

The incident response plan establishes an operational and strategic governance structure for managing cybersecurity incidents.

### Incident Response Governance

Two complementary management structures are defined:

| Team | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Incident Management Team (IMT) | Operational incident management, technical investigation, containment, eradication and recovery. |
| Senior Executive Management Team (SEMT) | Strategic oversight, executive decision-making, resource allocation and stakeholder engagement. |

The plan also defines responsibilities for the GRC Lead, CISO, Legal, Corporate Communications, Business Continuity, Security Operations and external service providers.

### Incident Classification and Escalation

The plan defines four incident categories based on organizational impact.

| Category | Impact Level | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Cyber Event | 0 | Suspected or unconfirmed security incident. |
| Cyber Incident | 1 | Confirmed security compromise with limited organizational impact. |
| Significant Cyber Incident | 2–3 | Major security incident requiring coordinated management and executive involvement. |
| Cyber Emergency | 4–5 | Enterprise-wide cyber crisis requiring immediate executive activation. |

Each category includes notification requirements, escalation triggers and management responsibilities.

### Incident Response Lifecycle

The plan establishes five response stages:

1. **Detection and Analysis:** Identify suspicious activity, confirm incidents, assess severity and activate appropriate response teams.
2. **Containment and Eradication:** Limit incident impact, remove threats and preserve relevant evidence.
3. **Communications and Engagement:** Coordinate internal and external stakeholder notifications.
4. **Recovery:** Restore affected systems, validate functionality and monitor for recurring threats.
5. **Learn and Improve:** Conduct post-incident reviews, document lessons learned and update response procedures.

### Executive and Stakeholder Engagement

The plan establishes notification responsibilities and escalation timeframes for:

- Senior executive management.
- Board of Directors and Audit and Risk Committee.
- Legal and Privacy teams.
- Employees, customers and business partners.
- Regulators and law enforcement.
- Cyber insurance providers.
- Media and public communications.

Notification requirements are subject to applicable legal and contractual obligations.

### Digital Forensics and Evidence Preservation

The plan establishes procedures for collecting, documenting, securing and transferring digital evidence.

Evidence management requirements include:

- Designated evidence custodians.
- Evidence collection and storage records.
- Controlled access to collected evidence.
- Chain-of-custody documentation.
- Recording evidence transfers and access rationale.
- Coordination with external digital forensics specialists where necessary.

### Continuous Improvement

The plan establishes annual review and tabletop exercise requirements, alongside post-incident reviews following significant cybersecurity incidents.

Corrective actions and lessons learned are intended to inform future revisions of the incident response plan.

## Supporting Operational Templates

The plan includes five operational templates:

| Template | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Situation Update | Communicate incident status, scope, impact and severity. |
| Incident Log | Record incident activities, decisions and supporting rationale. |
| Resolution Action Plan | Track containment, eradication, recovery and communication activities. |
| Evidence Register | Document evidence collection, storage, access and chain of custody. |
| Assets and Key Contacts | Maintain critical infrastructure information and response contacts. |

## Project Attribution and Limitations

This project was completed as a scenario-based practical assessment through GRC Mastery, with AI-assisted analysis and documentation.

Oscorp Industries is a simulated organization. Organizational roles, personnel, contact information and governance arrangements are illustrative.

The plan represents a proposed incident response framework rather than an implemented or independently tested enterprise incident response capability.

No actual cybersecurity incident was investigated or managed as part of this project.

The original training materials and official assessment solutions are excluded from this repository.
